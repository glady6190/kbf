Terms & Conditions

Kerala Badminton Federation UK

1. Acceptance of Terms

By registering with Kerala Badminton Federation UK (KBF-UK), you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by these Terms and Conditions.

2. Accuracy of Information

You declare that:

  • All information provided is accurate, complete, and genuine
  • Your personal details are truthful and verifiable
  • You will update any changes promptly

Warning

Providing false or misleading information is strictly prohibited and will result in immediate action.

3. Membership Approval

  • Membership is subject to administrative approval
  • Your Player ID is provisional until approval
  • Administrators may accept or reject applications
  • You'll be notified via email

Important

Until approved, you cannot participate in KBF-UK tournaments or events.

4. Consequences of Incorrect Information

False information will result in:

Immediate Disqualification

From all tournaments and events

Membership Revocation

Suspension of your membership and Player ID

Disciplinary Action

Warnings, fines, or permanent bans

Zero Tolerance

KBF-UK maintains strict policies against fraudulent registration.

5. Code of Conduct

As a member, you agree to:

  • Maintain sportsmanship and fair play
  • Respect officials, players, and rules
  • Compete honestly and ethically

6. Your information and how we use it

When you register, you agree that KBF-UK may use the information you give us, and your tournament results, to run your membership and the KBF grading system.

What we use it for

  • To review your registration and give you a KBF ID.
  • To grade you, record your tournament results and points, and decide promotions between grades.
  • To show you on the public Grading pages and in tournament results.
  • To contact you about your registration, your grade, tournaments and KBF news.

What everyone can see on the website

Your name, KBF ID, city, grade, season and career points, finals reached, tournament results, and your photo if you upload one. When you are promoted, we may announce it on the News page with your name and photo.

What stays private

Your email address, mobile number, post code and year of birth are never shown publicly. Only KBF administrators who need them can see them.

Who else sees your information

Tournament organisers see your name and KBF ID so they can record results. We do not sell or share your information with anyone else, apart from the companies that host our website and send our emails, who handle it only on our behalf.

Emails

We email you about your registration and your grade. You can stop KBF news emails at any time using the unsubscribe link in any of them.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you are a member. If you ask to leave, we delete your contact details and remove your name and photo from the website and our records. Past tournament results may be kept without your name. Some records are kept for a set time only: internet (IP) addresses in our logs for 90 days, the text of emails we send you for 30 days, our activity records for 2 years, and the details on an application we turned down for 1 year. When your application is approved, the copy of your details on the application is cleared once it is on your membership.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or object to how we use it, by emailing admin@kbf-uk.org. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Players under 18

If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must complete the registration and agree to these terms for you.

Declaration

By completing registration, you confirm understanding and acceptance of all terms. False information may result in disqualification and disciplinary action. Membership is subject to admin approval.
